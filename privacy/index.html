Who is responsible

Timerlan Magsumov publishes Purrch: Pomodoro Timer. For privacy questions or requests about your personal data, use the privacy contact on this page.

Focus and settings stored on your phone

Purrch works without a Purrch account. Your focus sessions, statistics, current timer, sound and notification settings, room selections, rewards and cracker balance are stored on the device. The iPhone version uses the app’s Application Support files and local preferences. Focus advice is calculated locally. The reviewed iPhone source has no publisher analytics endpoint or advertising SDK and does not send these records to the publisher.

Purchases and backups

Apple processes iPhone purchases. StoreKit supplies product details and purchase entitlements for displaying prices, verifying Pro and restoring access. Purrch does not receive your card details. Apple applies its own privacy policy. When you export a backup, you choose where to save or share it using iOS. A cloud or sharing provider you choose may receive that file. Purrch reads the file you select for import.

Notifications and app blocking

If permitted, Purrch uses local notifications to alert you when an interval ends. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. If the verified release includes the optional Screen Time feature, it asks for individual authorization and uses the apps or categories you select to apply a system shield during focus. The selection and scheduling state stay on the device and are provided to system Screen Time APIs to apply the shield. The release decision is pending testing on a physical iPhone.

Contacting support

If you email support, the publisher receives your address, message and any details or attachments you choose to send via Gmail (Google). The information is used to respond and follow up on the reported problem. We retain support correspondence for 12 months after the case is closed. Do not send payment card details, passwords or a full backup unless specifically needed for a case.

Your choices and rights

You can delete local app data by deleting the app and remove exported files from wherever you saved them. Manage subscriptions with Apple. To request access to, correction of or deletion of support data, or to exercise other applicable rights, use the privacy contact below. Where applicable, you may also contact your data protection authority.

Android release

The Android release is in preparation. Before it becomes available, this policy and the Google Play Data safety entry will be checked against the release build, including billing, storage and backups, notification permission, any system blocking, SDKs and network traffic.
