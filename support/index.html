Support

Help with your focus timer, sounds and Purrch Pro.

How do I run a focus session?

Set the timer and start a focus block. Adjust focus and break lengths in Settings. On iPhone, notification delivery and sound depend on your iOS settings.

How do I change the sounds?

Use Ambient Sounds during a focus session. Rain, campfire and stream are available in the basic version. Pro unlocks the full set. Sound and volume settings are available in the app.

Where are my focus charts and tips?

Purrch records focus history for all users. With active Purrch Pro, open Stats for focus totals, period charts, your Pomodoro timeline and personalized suggestions. If access expires, your local history remains available to Stats when Pro is restored.

How do I restore Pro on iPhone?

Open Purrch Pro and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions can be managed in the Subscriptions section of your Apple Account.

What about Android?

Purrch for Android phones is in preparation. Google Play purchase and restoration instructions will be added after the Android release build has been verified.

How do I move my history to another device?

On iPhone, export a backup from Settings → Data and import the saved file on the other device. An app backup does not transfer the store entitlement. Restore Pro separately through the store.
